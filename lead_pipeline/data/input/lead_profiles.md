## Ground rules (read first)

1. **No fabrication.** Every fact below comes from the lead's own form answers or from public web research (dated 2026-08-04). Anything unknown is marked **"pending Hermes enrichment"** — never guessed.
2. **Provisional scoring:** only 3 of 8 dimensions can be scored pre-call from the engine answers (`capital_readiness` ← profit answer; `technical_familiarity` + `motivation` ← years answer). Provisional total max = 40/100. **Full A/B/C tiering happens only after the qualification call** (strategy §5). `call_priority` (1–4) is the pre-call ranking key.
3. **The two Telugu answers are NOT re-asked on the call** — confirmed instead (guide §2.3).
4. Research was run only for the top-10 `call_priority` 1 leads (1–2 searches each). All other leads' research fields remain "pending Hermes enrichment".
